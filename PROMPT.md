# PROMPT: Recreate TS Brands Zim Website

## Overview
Build a premium, dark-themed creative agency SPA for **TS Brands Zim** — a full-service creative house based in Harare, Zimbabwe. The site serves three distinct brands under one umbrella and uses WhatsApp as its primary conversion channel.

---

## Core Concept: Brand Trinity Ecosystem

Create **distinct, stylized hubs** for three core services, each with its own visual identity while maintaining overall brand cohesion:

| Brand | Service | Visual Identity |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| **TS GraphicsZim** | Design (logos, branding, print, packaging) | 🎨 Creative, bold, typographic |
| **Treta Semah Media** | Photography (portraits, events, weddings, product) | 📸 Elegant, warm, editorial |
| **TS Clothing** | Apparel (t-shirts, hoodies, caps, corporate merch) | 👕 Streetwear, modern, product-focused |

Each hub has its own hero banner, portfolio gallery, and service-specific CTAs.

---

## Tech Stack
- **Vanilla JavaScript** — No frameworks. Custom Web Components only.
- **Hash-based SPA Routing** — `#home`, `#graphics`, `#photography`, `#clothing`, `#portfolio`, `#about`, `#contact`
- **Swiper.js** — Hero sliders and testimonials
- **Font Awesome 6** — Icons
- **Google Fonts: Inter** — Typography
